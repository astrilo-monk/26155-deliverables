How It Works

01

Upload

Ingest single or bulk CLI config files from any vendor - Cisco, FortiGate, Juniper, Palo Alto, or completely unknown.

02

Normalize

Vendor detection, dedicated parsers for known vendors, generic tokenizer + AI for the rest. All mapped to security facts.

03

Evaluate

15 security controls evaluated against CIS, NIST SP 800-53, DISA STIGs & ISO 27001. Posture & coverage scored.

04

Remediate & Report

Device-specific CLI fix commands, verified by re-scan. Per-device PDF report with Pass/Fail, risk severity, and remediation paths.
